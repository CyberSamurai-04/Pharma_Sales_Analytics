Data analytics case study

Six years of pharmacy sales, turned into a decision-ready pipeline

A raw Kaggle export cleaned in pandas, modeled and queried in SQL, and rendered into charts a category manager could act on the same afternoon. Built end to end as a self-directed analytics project.

5.75 yrs Coverage — 2 Jan 2014 to 8 Oct 2019
49.4% of all units held by one category
16,848 fact rows — date × category, long format
8 classes drug categories, mapped to ATC codes

Approach

How the data moves

Five stages, each one a real file in the repo — raw CSVs in, charts out. No step is skipped or simulated.

01
Raw CSVs
4 Kaggle exports at hourly, daily, weekly and monthly grain.
data/raw/archive/
02
clean_data.py
Parses 3 different date formats, drops a junk column, zero-fills, dedupes.
pandas
03
Cleaned CSVs
6 tidy files, including a long-format fact table and an ATC lookup.
data/cleaned/
04
sql_queries.py
Loads 6 tables into SQLite, indexes the fact table, runs 4 analytical queries.
pharma_sales.db
05
visualize.py
Reads the same queries back out, renders three charts to file.
matplotlib · seaborn
